# Rear-facing cinema seats and animated arrival

App uses `dist/assets/cinema-seats-rear.webp` and `dist/assets/cinema-milo-rear.webp`; matching PNG masters are saved in this folder. Two separate transparent assets let Milo swim in, beat his tail and settle behind the shell back, both seats facing the screen. Tap the seats to replay. Reduced-motion preference shows him seated immediately.

## Seat prompt

Create a transparent cutout of TWO matching open giant pearlescent clamshell cinema chairs side by side. CRITICAL ORIENTATION: CAMERA IS BEHIND BOTH CHAIRS. Both seats face AWAY from viewer toward an implied movie screen beyond the top of the image. We see the OUTSIDE BACK SURFACES of their scalloped fan-shaped raised upper valves, NOT their concave interiors, NOT the seat cushions. They are clearly turned away from us. Their rounded low bowl bases support the open shells, open at around 100 degrees, viewed from rear slightly above. Both parallel, same orientation, backs in foreground, small gap between. No person, no shark, no character, no head, no screen, no labels. Ivory nacre outer shell ridges with soft lavender teal iridescence and subtle golden edges, premium 3D animated-film cosy underwater cinema style. Low comfortable shell backrests, not huge towering shells. Both complete chairs fit in LOWER TWO THIRDS of landscape 3:2 canvas with generous transparent empty space ABOVE so a character’s head can be layered behind a chair back by the app. Entire transparent background, no floor, no scene, no shadows outside shells. Friendly and luxurious. Remember strictly REAR VIEW, chairs face away toward screen.

## Milo prompt

Create a transparent cutout of Milo the blue-grey megalodon character in the reference, viewed strictly FROM BEHIND, facing directly AWAY from camera and slightly upward toward a movie screen. Back of rounded shark head, broad shoulders/nape and a small central dorsal fin, side pectoral fins relaxed at his sides, curved little tail low to the left. Pose will be animated swimming in then settling into a seashell chair, so show his full compact rear silhouette without any chair or environment. Recognisable rear-facing shark companion, no legs, no arms, no clothes. NO FACE, NO EYES, NO MOUTH visible. Subtle brass monocle rim/chain peeking at the far right edge of his head only. Friendly 3D animated film character, detailed soft blue-grey skin with watery caustic lighting, pale underside only glimpsed at fin edges. Full body contained in portrait-ish central composition of landscape3:2 canvas with generous transparent margins; tall upright rear pose like looking up at a film rather than horizontal side-on swimming. Isolated completely transparent background, no chair, no scene, no shadows, no glow outside body.
