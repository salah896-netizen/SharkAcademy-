# Milo’s reef helpers

Original character art generated for this app, with alpha transparency preserved. Full-size masters live beside this note as `crew-NAME-source.png`; the app uses optimised `dist/assets/crew-NAME.webp` files.

Willa the whale shark: reading and stories. Tiggy the tiger shark: counting and maths. Gizmo the goblin shark: shark science, with a fossil-pattern side activity. Dash the mako shark: writing. Milo remains Haytham’s companion. These are fictional characters based on real shark species; the encyclopedia continues to distinguish photos and fiction.

All four use side-profile poses facing left, natural shark fins, species colouring, soft caustic lighting, friendly eyes, and a transparent background. The app adds swimming arrivals, separate tail movements, speech cues and reactions. No external media feed is used in the cinema; the empty cinema is ready for family-supplied videos.
